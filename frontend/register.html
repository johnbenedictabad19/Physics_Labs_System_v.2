Data Privacy Consent
Republic Act No. 10173 — Data Privacy Act of 2012

The Data Privacy Act of 2012 (RA 10173) protects the fundamental human right to privacy and ensures the responsible handling of personal information.

What data we collect:

  • Full name (last, first, middle initial)
  • Student number or email address
  • Birthday (students only, for account recovery)
  • Password (encrypted and never stored in plain text)

How we use your data:

  • To create and manage your PHYSLAB account
  • To track your laboratory activity submissions and grades
  • For identity verification and account recovery

Your rights under RA 10173:

  • Right to be informed about data processing
  • Right to access your personal data
  • Right to correct inaccurate data
  • Right to erasure of personal data upon request

Your data is stored securely and will not be shared with third parties without your explicit consent, except as required by law.

Accuracy of Information
Truthfulness and Responsibility

By registering on PHYSLAB, you affirm that all information you have provided during the registration process is true, correct, and accurate to the best of your knowledge.

This includes:

  • Your full legal name as it appears on school records
  • Your official student number (for students)
  • Your correct birthday (used for password recovery)
  • A valid email address (for professors)

Consequences of false information:

  • Account suspension or permanent deactivation
  • Referral to school academic integrity office
  • Inability to recover your account if credentials are lost

If you need to correct your information after registration, please contact your professor or the system administrator.

Terms of Use
PHYSLAB Academic System — User Agreement

PHYSLAB is an academic laboratory management system developed exclusively for use at EARIST (Eulogio "Amang" Rodriguez Institute of Science and Technology). By creating an account, you agree to the following terms:

Acceptable Use:

  • Use PHYSLAB solely for authorized academic laboratory activities
  • Submit only your own original work for laboratory activities
  • Respect the intellectual property of other users
  • Use the system only during your enrolled academic year

Prohibited Activities:

  • Sharing your login credentials with other students
  • Attempting to access another user's account
  • Submitting false or plagiarized laboratory data
  • Using the system for any non-academic purpose
  • Attempting to manipulate grades or system data

Account Responsibility:

  • You are responsible for all activity under your account
  • Report any unauthorized access to your account immediately
  • Keep your password confidential at all times

Violations of these terms may result in account suspension and referral to the appropriate school authorities in accordance with EARIST's student handbook and academic integrity policies.
