FAQ

Questions, answered.

Does PHYSLAB really accept any DOCX lab sheet?
Yes. parser.py reads headings, tables, equations, images and form fields, then renders them as an interactive worksheet. Your existing lab sheets keep their exact structure — students just fill them in online.
How does real-time collaboration work?
Flask-SocketIO keeps every member of a group on the same document. Edits, cursors and attachments propagate in milliseconds. Drafts are auto-saved server-side so nothing is ever lost on refresh.
What about authentication and privacy?
JWT-backed sessions with bcrypt password hashing and role-based access control. Admin, professor and student scopes are enforced server-side on every route — a student never sees another group's data.
Can professors build activities without DOCX?
Yes. The manual activity builder offers the same question types — short answer, data tables, equation blocks, file prompts — with drag-to-reorder and per-section scoring.
What happens after the semester ends?
Classes archive cleanly for both professors and students. Past submissions, grades and feedback stay readable; admins manage retention.
How do invites and attendance work together?
Professors issue class codes or direct invites. Once a student joins, per-session attendance is a single tap during class — the invite system and roll-call share the same roster.
